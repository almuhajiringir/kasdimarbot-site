KasdimarBot

KasdimarBot is a private cash-book assistant. Its owner uses it to record income and expenses in Google Sheets and to store receipt photos in Google Drive. It is not a public service and has no public sign-up.

What it does with Google data

The app accesses only the Google Sheets spreadsheet and the Google Drive folder that the owner has shared with its Google account. It adds rows to the cash book and uploads receipt photos to that folder.
