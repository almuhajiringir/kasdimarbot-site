Privacy Policy

Back to KasdimarBot

KasdimarBot is a private application used only by its owner and a small number of authorized users to record income and expenses.

Google user data the app accesses

The app accesses Google Sheets spreadsheets and Google Drive files that the owner has explicitly shared with the app's Google account. It reads and writes the cash-book spreadsheet, and it uploads receipt photos to a designated Drive folder.

How the data is used

Data is used only to record and display cash-book entries and to store receipt photos as proof of transactions. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising.

Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

Spreadsheet and photo data stays in the owner's Google Sheets and Google Drive. Access tokens are stored on the owner's private server and are not shared.

Retention and removal

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and can delete the stored token from the server.
